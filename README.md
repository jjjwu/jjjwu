# Jun Wu

Independent engineer documenting reproducible Wi-Fi CSI experiments on commodity routers, OpenWrt, and mt76.

## Public work

- [Router CSI Lab](https://csi.okwujun.pw/) - bilingual engineering notes, measurements, and build logs for GL-MT6000 / MediaTek MT7986 CSI capture.
- [GL-MT6000 CSI lab log](https://csi.okwujun.pw/en/gl-mt6000-csi-lab-log/) - first-hand setup, capture quality, timestamp gaps, and throughput findings.
- [Router CSI Toolkit](https://github.com/jjjwu/router-csi-toolkit) - MIT-licensed capture, analysis, watchdog, and HE40/HE80 tuning scripts.
- [10-minute CSI dataset](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23434789) - anonymised raw capture, checksums, parser notes, and reproducible analysis.
- [Methods and corrections](https://csi.okwujun.pw/en/methodology/) - evidence boundaries, measurement assumptions, and the public correction log.

## Research scope

Commodity Wi-Fi hardware, CSI capture reliability, reproducible measurement, and privacy-aware wireless sensing. The material is for research and education, not medical use or non-consensual surveillance.
